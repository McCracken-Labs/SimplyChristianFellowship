Simply Christian Fellowship

To follow the teachings of Christ is Simply to Love

A place for people seeking a clear and sincere walk with Christ. No memberships, no titles, no expectations, just a shared desire to learn, grow, and live out the teachings of Jesus together.

Who We Are What We Believe

A Calm, Open Space

This is a place to explore faith without pressure or judgment. It is united by a simple commitment to follow Christ with honesty, compassion, and love.

Many people love Christ but feel disconnected from organized religion. Some have experienced confusion, burnout, or harm in institutional settings. Others want a faith that is direct, peaceful, and centered on the teachings of Jesus Himself. Simply Christian Fellowship offers a quiet space for learning, reflection, and connection.
