About Us

A sincere walk with Christ

Simply Christian Fellowship exists to help people follow Jesus in a clear, simple, and sincere way — free from guilt, pressure, or hierarchy.

Simply Christian Fellowship exists to help people follow Jesus in a clear, simple, and sincere way. This is a place for anyone who wants to focus on Christ without the added weight of religious systems, traditions, or expectations. The goal is to support people who seek an honest relationship with Christ, free from guilt, pressure, or hierarchy.

Many people love Christ but feel disconnected from organized religion. Some have experienced confusion, burnout, or harm in institutional settings. Others simply want a faith that is direct, peaceful, and centered on the teachings of Jesus Himself. Simply Christian Fellowship offers a calm space for learning, reflection, and connection for anyone walking this path.

Scripture as a window, not an idol

Scripture is treated as a collection of writings from many different people across different eras, giving a glimpse into the world before Christ and the world shaped by His arrival. These texts show a chaotic history marked by laws, customs, and human struggle, followed by the simplicity and clarity of life presented in the New Testament. Nothing in these writings is holy in itself. Only Christ is holy. Scripture helps reveal the world He entered and the way He taught His followers to live. Through the words of the New Testament authors, we see the character, compassion, and direction Christ intended for those who follow Him.

Giving is love, never a requirement

We reject the idea that financial giving determines spiritual standing. The tithe system is not a requirement for belonging to Christ, nor is it a measure of faith. Giving should never be tied to salvation, guilt, or institutional obligation. Christ never demanded a percentage — He asked for a heart aligned with love, compassion, and service. Faith expressed through generosity is meaningful only when it is free, sincere, and without pressure.

Not an institution — a shared walk

Simply Christian Fellowship is not an organization built on titles or positions, and does not believe any one person, place, or thing is holy. No man is created above any other. This is a space for people to learn, grow, and reconnect with Christ honestly. As the community develops, more voices, experiences, and insights will shape it, while keeping the foundation unchanged: Christ at the center, simplicity in practice, and love as the expression of faith. Faith without works is lost. We are not Christians because we say we are. We are simply Christians by living out Christ’s teachings in the way we love, serve, and care for others.

Fellowship Unifies

Held together by love, not sameness

Fellowship is simply people walking toward Christ together. It does not require agreement on every question, membership in an organization, or sameness of background. It asks only for shared honesty and shared love.

For too long, faith has been used to divide — by denomination, by politics, by who is in and who is out. Jesus moved in the opposite direction. He ate with the excluded, crossed every social line of His day, and prayed that His followers would be one. Unity, for Him, was never uniformity. It was love strong enough to hold difference without fear.

Here, fellowship means you are welcome as you are — with your questions, your doubts, and your story. We come from different places and see many things differently, and that is not a threat. What holds us together is not a creed to sign but a way to walk: Christ at the center, compassion in practice, and love as the language we share.
